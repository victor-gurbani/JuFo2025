# BLL Colloquium Preparation

Target format assumption: ~20 minutes total  
- 10 minutes presentation  
- 10 minutes defense discussion

## 1. Presentation Structure (10 minutes)

## 1.1 Minute-by-minute flow

1. `0:00-1:00` Hook + relevance
- Why this problem matters in school operations and safety.

2. `1:00-2:00` Leitfrage + hypotheses
- State research question clearly and exactly once.

3. `2:00-4:00` Method
- Explain comparison setup and chosen metrics.
- Mention alternatives and why they were not selected.

4. `4:00-7:00` Results
- Show key figures/tables.
- Focus only on decisive evidence.

5. `7:00-8:30` Limits and critical reflection
- What failed, what remains uncertain, what would be changed next.

6. `8:30-10:00` Answer to Leitfrage + outlook
- Direct, evidence-based conclusion.

## 2. Thesenpapier Quality Rules

- Each thesis must be assertive, not descriptive.
- Each thesis must be debatable (not obvious facts).
- Each thesis must have direct evidence anchor (chapter/figure/metric).
- Keep language compact and defensible.

## 3. Defense Question Bank

## 3.1 Method-choice questions

- Why did you select this method over alternatives?
- Which assumptions were required for your method to work?
- Which tradeoffs were accepted knowingly?

## 3.2 Data and validity questions

- How representative is your test data for real school operations?
- Which bias risks exist in your dataset?
- How reproducible are your results?

## 3.3 Limitation and reflection questions

- What is the most serious weakness of your current approach?
- Which result is least stable and why?
- If you had 6 more months, what would you redesign first?

## 3.4 Interdisciplinary questions

- Where are the legal/privacy boundaries of this system?
- What is the ethical risk of false positives/false negatives?
- How does this affect school workflow and staffing?

## 4. Answer Framework (Use In Defense)

Use this structure for each answer:

1. `Position`  
Give direct answer in one sentence.

2. `Evidence`  
Reference concrete metric/chapter/table.

3. `Limitation`  
State known constraint honestly.

4. `Implication`  
Explain practical consequence and next action.

## 5. Rehearsal Checklist

- [ ] 10-minute version fits without rushing.
- [ ] No slide is read verbatim.
- [ ] Every key claim has a numeric or documented anchor.
- [ ] At least 2 difficult defense questions rehearsed per topic block.
- [ ] Closing statement answers Leitfrage directly.

## 6. Final Colloquium Packet

- [ ] Presentation slides.
- [ ] Printed Thesenpapier.
- [ ] One-page evidence map (thesis -> chapter/figure).
- [ ] Backup media/device plan.
- [ ] Quick glossary of technical terms for oral clarity.
